01 — The Settlement Gap

Trials make headlines.
Settlements make markets.

The overwhelming majority of civil cases never see a courtroom. They end in conference rooms, over numbers negotiated from experience, instinct, and incomplete information.

Yet the data describing those outcomes — what cases actually settle for, by type, by jurisdiction, by fact pattern — remains scattered across dockets, memories, and filing cabinets. The most consequential pricing information in the legal economy has no public benchmark.

SettlementValuation.com names the discipline that closes that gap. It is the natural home for the platform, dataset, or practice that turns settlement from folklore into finance.
